Terms of Service

Last updated: 1 October 2026

What GesTeach is

GesTeach is a personal tool for a teacher's own planning and record-keeping: modules and topics, a lesson diary, a register of grades and observations, and an archive of materials. It is not an official school record system and does not replace your school's register. What it holds is your working copy.

Your account

One account belongs to one person. Keep your password to yourself, and treat what is done under your account as done by you. If you sign in with Google, that sign-in is also governed by Google's own terms.

The data you enter is your responsibility

You decide what to record about your pupils, and you answer for it to your school and under data-protection law. Record only what you are permitted to record, and remove what you no longer need.

Your data lives on your device

Unless you turn on the cloud backup, there is no copy of your register on any server, and nobody can restore it for you: a phone lost, broken or reset to factory settings, or an app uninstalled, takes the data with it. Export a backup regularly and keep it somewhere other than the device it came from, or turn on the cloud backup. That copy is encrypted with a secret phrase only you know: if you lose both the secret phrase and the recovery code, nobody — the app's authors included — can open it or give it back to you. It is a snapshot taken when a backup runs, not a live copy: what you entered after the last backup is not in it.

No warranty

The app is provided as it is, without warranty of any kind. Averages, proposed grades, hour counts and reminders are conveniences computed from what you entered: check them against your own judgement before using them for anything official.

Limits of liability

To the extent the law allows, the app's authors are not liable for lost or corrupted data, for a backup that cannot be read, or for decisions taken on the basis of figures the app shows. Nothing written here limits rights that cannot be limited by agreement.

Services the app uses

Signing in uses Firebase Authentication and the cloud backup, if you turn it on, uses Cloud Storage for Firebase and Firebase App Check: all are Google services with terms and privacy policies of their own. The app uses no other network service.

Ending

You can sign out at any time, and delete your account from the Account page. Deleting the account also deletes your cloud backup and every class on this device — export them to a file first if you want to keep them.

Changes

These terms change with the app; the date at the top says when they last did.
