Featured project

GesTeach, the app for teachers

Modules and topics, with the scheduled assessments inside each module. The hours update themselves from the lessons in the diary.

Every lesson with its date, length and module, as a list or on the month's calendar, together with assessments and unexpected events.

Grades, averages by term and observations, pupil by pupil. A grade takes two taps.

Slides, tests, rubrics, videos and exercises, in one folder per module. PDFs and videos open inside the app.

How we work

Three steps,
from idea to app

  1. 01

    We listen to the people who will use it.

    We start from their days: what they need to do, what wastes their time, what must never happen to their data.

  2. 02

    We build.

    Apps that work without a network and keep the data on the device. No advertising, no tracking.

  3. 03

    We release and improve.

    Small, frequent versions, tried before they go out. Every report goes back into the work.
