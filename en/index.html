Why GesTeach Your data stays yours

Made for school, starting with the data

Names, grades and observations often belong to minors: GesTeach keeps them on the device and never sends them anywhere in a form someone else could read.

  • Works offline

    Lessons, grades and materials can be read and written without a network.

  • Data stays on the device

    Classes, pupils and grades never pass through our servers, and nothing is synced.

  • Encrypted backup, if you want it

    The cloud backup is optional and leaves already encrypted: only you can open it.

  • App lock

    Fingerprint, face or a code: a tablet left on the desk does not show the register to whoever picks it up.

  • Export whenever you like

    Grades as CSV or as a printable PDF, and your classes in a backup file you keep.

  • No trackers

    No advertising, and no analytics or tracking library inside the app.
