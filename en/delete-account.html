Deleting your GesTeach account

GesTeach, developed by AM Studio

From the app

  1. Open GesTeach and tap the account icon, the last one at the top right (it is also on the classes screen and on the subscription screen).
  2. The Account page opens: scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. In the Delete this account? window, enter your password if you sign in with email and password; if you sign in with Google, you may be asked to pick your Google account again.
  4. Tap Delete.

Deletion is immediate: your cloud backup is deleted first, if you turned it on, then the account, then every class on that device. If the backup cannot be deleted, the account is not deleted either and the app tells you so, so that no backup is ever left without an owner.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app or the device, write to am.projects.team@gmail.com with the subject “GesTeach account deletion”, from the email address you sign in with (for a Google account, that account's Gmail address): that is how we know the request comes from you. Nothing else is needed, and we never ask for your password.

We reply and delete the account within 30 days of the request, and write to you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • The account on Firebase Authentication (Google): email address, password in hashed form, the display name you chose, the identifiers Google supplies if you sign in with Google, and the dates of your sign-ins.
  • The cloud backup, if you turned it on: every file of the encrypted backup on Cloud Storage for Firebase, the key file included.

What is kept

Nothing is kept on our servers: we keep no copy of the account or of the backup after deletion.

Your teaching data (classes, pupils, grades, lessons, materials) was never on our servers: it lives only on your device. Deleting the account from the app deletes it there too; if you ask for deletion by email, it goes when you uninstall the app. Backup or export files you saved yourself stay where you put them, and are yours to delete.

Deleting only the cloud backup

To delete the cloud backup and keep the account: Account › Cloud backup › Turn off and delete. The backup is deleted at once; the data on the device stays.
