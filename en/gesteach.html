GesTeach

GesTeach is an Android app for teachers: planning by modules and topics, a lesson diary, a register of grades and observations, and an archive of materials, class by class.

Your teaching data stays on your device and the app works without a network. The account is used only to sign in and, if you turn it on, for an encrypted cloud backup that only you can open. No advertising, no tracking.

The four areas

One class, four sections

  • Programming

    Modules and topics, with the scheduled assessments inside each module. The hours update themselves from the lessons in the diary.

  • Lesson diary

    Every lesson with its date, length and module, as a list or on the month's calendar, together with assessments and unexpected events.

  • Pupils and grades

    Grades, averages by term and observations, pupil by pupil. A grade takes two taps.

  • Materials

    Slides, tests, rubrics, videos and exercises, in one folder per module. PDFs and videos open inside the app.
