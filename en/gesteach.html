GesTeach A project by AM Studio

The app for teachers, class by class

Planning by modules and topics, a lesson diary, a register of grades and observations, and an archive of materials. Your teaching data stays on your device and the app works without a network. The account is used only to sign in and, if you turn it on, for an encrypted cloud backup that only you can open. No advertising, no tracking.

01 Programming

Planning, module by module

Modules and topics, with the scheduled assessments inside each module. The hours update themselves from the lessons in the diary.

  • A module's start and end, even as just a month
  • Written and oral assessments, each with its date
  • A plan can be copied from one class to another

02 Lesson diary

The diary, as a list or on the month

Every lesson with its date, length and module, as a list or on the month's calendar, together with assessments and unexpected events.

  • Each lesson's hours count towards its module
  • Lessons and assessments on the same calendar
  • Unexpected events noted on the right day

03 Pupils and grades

The register, pupil by pupil

Grades, averages by term and observations, pupil by pupil. A grade takes two taps.

  • Averages by term and a proposed mark
  • The list of pupils is imported from a CSV file
  • Grades are exported as CSV or as a printable PDF

04 Materials

Every material in its module's folder

Slides, tests, rubrics, videos and exercises, in one folder per module. PDFs and videos open inside the app.

  • Files from the device or from Google Drive, with the system picker
  • Google Docs, Sheets and Slides through their sharing link
  • PDFs and videos open without leaving GesTeach
